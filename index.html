About Us

Madrasatul Luqman is a center of learning dedicated to authentic Islamic knowledge and excellence in character. We serve learners across primary and secondary levels with integrated dini and secular curricula.

  • Qualified ulamaa and certified teachers
  • Memorization (Hifdh) and Tajweed
  • Integrated KICD curriculum
  • Safe, nurturing environment
